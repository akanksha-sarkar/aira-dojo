UNDERSTANDING BIOLOGY-SPECIFIC IMAGE DATA
------
Biology images often have different characteristics than standard computer vision datasets:

**Image Resolution and Object Scales:**
- Biology images are frequently high-resolution/bigger in pixel size, with image sizes up to 8K (7680 x 4320) being common.
- Objects of interest (animals, cells) may occupy only a small number of pixels within these large images.
- **Important:** Aggressive downsampling can destroy critical information when objects are small. Carefully consider whether resizing is necessary and what resolution preserves the details needed for detection.
- If resizing, consider whether the chosen resolution maintains sufficient detail for small objects.
